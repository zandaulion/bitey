# Bitey 1.1.2 (`versionCode` 19) — Play release notes

Play Console takes all languages in one box, each wrapped in its locale tag;
`release-notes-1.1.2.xml` beside this file is that paste-ready form, and
this copy is generated from it. Play allows 500 characters per locale; the
longest here, French, is 426.

1.1.2 makes the measured burn estimate steadier. The weight trend is fitted
over a window that grows to 42 days (it was a fixed 28, where a few heavy
mornings could move the figure by hundreds of kcal and spread its range across
more than 1,000); coverage is counted from the first logged day, so a new user
still gets a measurement after two weeks. The profile formula is weighed in by
how sure each side is, so the figure moves smoothly from formula to
measurement, and the range is one standard deviation, the formula's own
reading of "likely". Today is left out until it is over. The weight chart in
You fits its line to the same window and says since when, so it no longer
disagrees with the figure beside it.

## English (en-US)

A steadier read on what you burn.

- The range around what you burn is much narrower: Bitey looks at six weeks of weigh-ins and leans on your profile while the evidence builds
- Today no longer counts until it is over, so breakfast alone does not pull the estimate down
- The weight chart draws the same trend the estimate uses, and says since when
