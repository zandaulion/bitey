# Bitey 1.0.11 (`versionCode` 12) — Play release notes

Play Console takes all languages in one box, each wrapped in its locale tag;
`release-notes-1.0.11.xml` beside this file is that paste-ready form, and
this copy is generated from it. Play allows 500 characters per locale; the
longest here, Romanian, is 496.

1.0.11 is about finding and reading food. Manual entry searches a far larger
table (USDA plus ANSES CIQUAL), with 883 common foods and dishes named in all
twelve app languages and accent-free matching. Bitey AI counts loose foods
(grapes, berries, nuts) instead of judging a heap by eye, and keeps its own
weight next to any correction. A new Settings switch, off by default, sends
a vegetarian, vegan or pescatarian diet with each photo so an ambiguous dish
is read the way the person eats; the privacy notice of 5 October 2026
describes it. The typed-food form and the backup text are now translated.
Counting loose foods is server-side and already live for every version.

## English (en-US)

Find your food faster, in your language.

- A much bigger food table, now with European foods, and nearly 900 common foods and dishes named in every app language
- Bitey AI counts loose foods like grapes, berries and nuts, so a full bunch is no longer read as half of one
- New in Settings: let Bitey AI use your vegetarian, vegan or pescatarian diet for dishes that could be made either way. Off unless you turn it on
