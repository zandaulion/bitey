# Bitey 1.0.7 (`versionCode` 8) — Play release notes

Play Console takes all languages in one box, each wrapped in its locale tag;
`release-notes-1.0.7.xml` beside this file is that paste-ready form, and this
copy is generated from it. Play allows 500 characters per locale; the longest
here, French, is 416.

1.0.7 wires the two Bitey AI actions on a saved meal that the Android build
had never connected: "Not what you ate?" (read the photo again with a
correction) and "Photograph what's left" (compare the plate before and after).
Both used to end in "This local feature has not been connected yet". The
leftovers reading's server half is already live in the analysis function. The
page's confirmations and prompts now show as the phone's own dialogs instead of
WebView's "The page at https://plate.local says", and the messages they carry,
along with the "Not what you ate?" link itself, are now translated.

## English (en-US)

Bitey AI now works on meals you've already logged.

- "Not what you ate?" on a saved meal: tell Bitey AI what it really was and it reads the photo again
- "Photograph what's left": Bitey AI compares the plate before and after to work out how much you ate
- Confirmations now appear as your phone's own dialogs, in your language
