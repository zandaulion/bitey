# Bitey 1.0.12 (`versionCode` 13) — Play release notes

Play Console takes all languages in one box, each wrapped in its locale tag;
`release-notes-1.0.12.xml` beside this file is that paste-ready form, and
this copy is generated from it. Play allows 500 characters per locale; the
longest here, German, is 230.

1.0.12 makes the first Bitey AI reading after opening the app quick and
reliable. The App Check token is now fetched when the app starts and kept
fresh, instead of being requested when the photo is sent (a Play Integrity
attestation of up to 10 seconds, after which the request went without a token
and was refused). A photo taken before Play Billing had reported the
subscription now waits up to 5 seconds for it instead of being told Bitey AI
is not active. The app and the analysis function also log how long each step
of a reading took.
