# Bitey 1.0.9 (`versionCode` 10) — Play release notes

Play Console takes all languages in one box, each wrapped in its locale tag;
`release-notes-1.0.9.xml` beside this file is that paste-ready form, and this
copy is generated from it. Play allows 500 characters per locale; the longest
here, German, is 403.

1.0.9 offers the first month free. The `free-month` offers are active on both
base plans in Play Console (new customer acquisition, "never had this
subscription", one phase: free trial, 1 month); earlier builds ignore them.
The plan picker shows "First month free · then {price}" wherever Play offers
the trial to that account, and the purchase takes the trial when Play still
offers it. It also puts what Bitey AI saw straight under the photo on the
review sheet: each food with its weight and calories, and their total, each
one a jump to its full row.

Your first month of Bitey AI is free.
